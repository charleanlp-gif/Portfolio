EGD TutorBoard

Online technical drawing platform for EGD tutoring

EGD is difficult to tutor online because normal whiteboards do not provide the specialised drawing tools the subject needs. TutorBoard was built around that problem, with digital rulers, triangles, protractor, compass tools, dimensioning and multi-page drawing support.
